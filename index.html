01 / About

From building software
to helping teams build well.

My work has grown beyond individual tasks to the wider system: the people, decisions and delivery practices behind useful software.

14+ years across software engineering and IT delivery

I began in software development, learning how systems are built and what it takes to make them reliable. Working with Japanese customers and Vietnamese engineering teams broadened my view: good delivery depends as much on shared understanding as it does on good code.

Over time, my focus expanded into requirements, planning, team coordination and customer communication. I enjoy turning a complex situation into clear priorities, practical next steps and a team that can move forward together.

The question I keep coming back to is simple: how can a team create something that genuinely serves the business?

Engineering→ BrSE→ Coordination→ Delivery
